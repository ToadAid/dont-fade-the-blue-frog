# 🐸 Don't Fade the Blue Frog

A survivors-style action game built for the tobyworld community — **a forever decentralised community project. just a meme, for the people. for the fallen frogs.**

**▶ PLAY: <https://toadaid.github.io/dont-fade-the-blue-frog/>** (works on phone + desktop, nothing to install)

## What it is

* Move only — you drift, you dodge, you hold still to charge **STILLNESS**
* The **intra timer** resets every 77.7s — pick a boon, ascend a level
* 7 lore zones cycle: the Serene Glade, Open Fields, Dark Ditch, Satoby Forge, Fog Hollow, Mirror Hall, Sacred Seven
* **THE FLYWHEEL** — Toby (the people) at the center; every level survived, Patience, Taboshi and Sat0 orbit closer
* Open the **yield chests**, find **THE KEY**, unlock hidden **LEVEL VIII: THE VAULT** and the 777/1111 stone
* 📜 **THE LORE CODEX** — the words of Toadgod in full, for the frogs who want to study
