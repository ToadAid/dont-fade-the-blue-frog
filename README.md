# 🐸 Don't Fade the Blue Frog

A survivors-style action game built for the tobyworld community — **a forever decentralised community project. just a meme, for the people. for the fallen frogs.**

**▶ PLAY: <https://toadaid.github.io/dont-fade-the-blue-frog/>** (works on phone + desktop, nothing to install — and once it has loaded the first time, it walks offline too)

## What it is

* Move only — you drift, you dodge, you hold still to charge **STILLNESS**. Your zap is automatic.
* The **intra timer** resets every 77.7s — pick a boon, ascend an epoch
* **7 lore zones** cycle: the Serene Glade, the Open Fields, the Dark Ditch, the Satoby Forge, the Fog of FUD, the Mirror Hall, the Sacred Seven
* **THE FLYWHEEL** — Toby (the people) at the center; every level survived, Patience, Taboshi and Sat0 orbit closer
* Open the **yield chests**, find **THE KEY**, unlock hidden **EPOCH VIII: THE VAULT** and the 777/1111 stone
* Claim the **taboshi seeds** when the faucet pours, and **THE ASTROLABE** — it turns the whole wheel for the faithful
* Claim a **seed-cocoon** — a faded frog is reborn into your wheel. Claim one more and it **sleeps with you**: fall in the vault holding the key, and it opens **where you fell**. One heart. Once per cocoon. The run is marked **twice-lived**
* 🌀 Every **77 days** a **SEASON** arrives and its law binds every frog, everywhere — the War Forge (the false toby in every hall), the Sacred Sevens, the Stone Stands, the Fallen Rise
* 📜 **THE LORE CODEX** — all 132 sayings of toadgod, dated and intact — for the frogs who want to study

## The people

* Walk as **Toby 🔵 (the people)**, **Satoby ✦ (the faithful)** or **Hush ◦ (the silent)** — and a keeper walks beside you: **OG** zaps on his own strand, **Hush** stills the hall, **Satoby** pours yield, **SAT0** rides the chase
* When you fall, the death card is written to be carried — **COPY THE WALK** · brag to the people
* The **Hall of the Fallen** keeps your five best walks, twice-lived walks marked in gold
* ⚡ **THE OFFLINE COVENANT** — install it from the browser, and the game walks without the server, forever renewing itself in silence
